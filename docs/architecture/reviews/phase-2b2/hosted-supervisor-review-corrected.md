## New residual risk (mine, severity: MEDIUM — must fix before pilot)

- **R1 — pid signaling without process-identity check (reuse/wrong-pid hazard).** `stopPidWithProof` and the `stopWorkerRuntime` external branch signal whatever `record.pid` says; my probe shows a fabricated pid record (`999999`, never a child) resolves as "confirmed" because `pidAlive` is false. In the threat model that matters: (a) a stale record after unclean pid reuse could SIGTERM/SIGKILL an unrelated recycled process; (b) a malicious or corrupted mapping row (HostedStore is a local SQLite file) becomes a local signal primitive. Mitigations available: verify identity before signaling (record child start-time/`btime` or `/proc/<pid>/starttime`, or keep a socket-pair/lockfd inheritance check); refuse to signal pids never spawned by this supervisor lineage without operator confirmation; treat "pid absent at first check with no prior liveness observation" as hold-with-budget rather than instant confirmed-stop when the record's provenance is weak. The conservative `markHeldUnknown` budget already bounds the accounting side; the signaling side needs the identity check.
- **R2 — LOW: `performance.now()` monotonic basis.** Monotonic within a process, but pid/start records compared across restarts mix process clocks; elapsed math is only used post-start in-process or bounded by reservation — acceptable, noted for the record.
- **R3 — LOW: loop `Bun.sleep(min(authority+500, 30000))` blocks the producer loop per task** (no concurrent tasks, no renew-during-wait beyond internal timers). Fine for the controlled pilot (maxTasks=1 default); document as a scaling limit.
